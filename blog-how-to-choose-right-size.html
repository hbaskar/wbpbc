How to Choose the Right Size for Your Pickleball Court

When planning to build a pickleball court in your backyard, one of the most important factors to consider is the size of the court. The right dimensions ensure a comfortable playing experience while fitting seamlessly into your space.

Standard Court Dimensions

A regulation-size pickleball court measures 20 feet wide by 44 feet long, which works for both singles and doubles play. If your backyard space is limited, this size is typically a great option.

Expanding the Space

If you have additional space and want to create a more spacious environment, we recommend expanding to 30 feet by 60 feet. This gives you extra buffer zones around the court, which are ideal for movement and safety during high-intensity play.

Factors to Consider

Consider your available yard space, how many players you expect, and your long-term goals for the court. At We Build Pickleball Courts, we’ll guide you through every decision to ensure your court is functional and enjoyable.
