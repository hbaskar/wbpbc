Our Court Construction Process

Introduction Phase

Meet your Client Relations Manager to conduct survey of pickleball court location and choose ideal pickleball court dimensions. Receive detailed quote based on square foot.

Pre-Construction Phase

Our Client Relations Manager will hand off to the construction team. Preparing documents & permits as well as a custom multi-sport court diagram.

Construction Phase

The court site is prepped, materials are sourced, vapor barrier and concrete base is completed, followed by prep work for the color coatings.

Cure Times & Surface Preparation

Choose your Court Colors with our online tool. Chosen Sport lines are painted and the playing surface is prepped for competition with a acrylic surface. Additional Custom Lines added for multi-court functionality.

Hand Over to Client

The multi-court courts are ready for use by any skill level! First timers to pros!
