What Are The Dimensions Of A Pickleball Court?

To get the most from your backyard pickleball court, selecting the right layout and size is essential. The standard court size is 20 feet wide by 44 feet long, perfect for both singles and doubles play. This setup provides enough room for an exciting, competitive game while fitting comfortably into most residential backyards.

If you have more space available, we recommend expanding to 30 feet by 60 feet. This option includes buffer zones around the court, giving players extra room to move safely and comfortably. These zones are ideal for active play, family matches, and enhancing the overall playing experience.

Whether you prefer a compact setup or a full-size layout, a backyard pickleball court brings daily fun, fitness, and family connection. Our team handles everything—from design and layout to surfacing and finishing touches.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the dimensions of a standard pickleball court?

A standard pickleball court measures 20 ft wide by 44 ft long for both singles and doubles. However, we can accommodate adjustments based on available space and specific requirements. Our typical standalone pickleball courts are 30 feet by 60 feet with 30 feet by 60 feet also being an ideal starting point for multi-use courts.

Can you customize the design of the pickleball court to fit our specific space and preferences?

We specialize in customizing pickleball court designs to suit your space and preferences. Our team works closely with you to create a court design that meets your needs and multi-use preferences.

How long does it typically take to construct a pickleball court from start to finish?

The construction time can vary depending on the scope and complexity of the project. However, on average, it takes approximately 4-6 weeks to complete a pickleball court from start to finish.

What kind of fencing is suitable for a pickleball court, and how does it contribute to player safety?

We offer various fencing options for pickleball courts, including chain-link and mesh fencing. Fencing enhances player safety by preventing balls from leaving the court area and providing a secure playing environment.

Is there an ideal orientation for the pickleball court?

Yes, for outdoor courts, the court location should be ideally placed north-to-south, as seen in local parks and multi-court complexes.

Can you build a pickleball court in your backyard?

You can build a backyard pickleball court if you have a flat, level area with drainage and enough clearance around it. Our expert team will come visit your site in-person to measure the feasibility and ensure we can fit the full pickleball court dimensions.

Can you convert a tennis court to a pickleball court?

Yes, you can convert a tennis court to a pickleball court by adding lines for shared use or dedicated layouts, as tennis courts often fit multiple pickleball courts.

Can you resurface an existing pickleball court?

Absolutely. We offer pickleball court resurfacing for existing courts, repairing the court surface and applying a premium acrylic coating to make it look and play like a professional pickleball court. It’s a cost-effective way to get a pro-level surface without a full rebuild. Get a free quote today and bring your court back to life!

What is the average pickleball court construction cost?

The pickleball court construction cost can vary depending on several factors, including location, court size, material choices, and whether it’s a residential or commercial project. For a more accurate estimate tailored to your specific needs, contact us for a free consultation.
