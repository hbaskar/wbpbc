What Are the Dimensions of a Full-Size Court?

We build outdoor courts in a variety of sizes to suit your available space and level of play. From compact to regulation, we’ll match your needs perfectly.

 

  • Full Size Court: 84’ x 50’ (Standard high school dimensions)

  • Half Courts: 30’ x 60’ (extended play space) or 30’ x 50’ (compact layout)

  • Mini Courts: 25’ x 20’ or 25’ x 25’

 

Not sure what fits? Our team will help determine the ideal court size and court dimensions for your own basketball court.

Is There More Than One 3-Point Line?

Absolutely! Depending on the level of play you want to replicate, we can install different 3-point arcs:

 

  • High School: 19’ 9” from the basket

  • College: 22’ 1¾” (recent update from previous 20’ 9”)

  • NBA/Pro: 23’ 9” at the top of the arc, 22’ in the corners

 

We can paint any of these to regulation—or even multiple arcs if you’d like to train across levels. We’ll help you create a high-performance practice space that suits any level of competition.

 

With our design team and our interactive court designer, we’ll make sure you get the best court for your home, recreation center, or commercial facility.
