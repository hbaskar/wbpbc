How Does It Work?

Pickleball courts are smaller than tennis courts, meaning you can fit multiple pickleball courts within a standard tennis court layout. The process involves:

Measuring and marking the correct dimensions for pickleball courts.

Applying durable, high-visibility lines that won’t interfere with tennis play.
Customizing colors to differentiate between sports while keeping a clean look.

At We Build Pickleball, we make the conversion process simple and efficient, so you can maximize your court space for both tennis and pickleball.
