Backyard Artificial Turf Installation: The Perfect Low-Maintenance Solution for Any Space

Natural grass looks great—until it doesn’t. Mowing, watering, and dealing with dead patches can quickly become a hassle. That’s where artificial turf comes in. Whether you want to enhance your patio, surround your pickleball court, install a putting green, or eliminate lawn maintenance altogether, We Build Pickleball Courts has the perfect turf option for you.

Different Turf Heights for Different Needs
Not all turf is created equal. The best choice depends on how you plan to use it:

Short & Dense (Putting Greens) – A smooth, tight-knit surface designed for golf enthusiasts who want a true roll.

Medium-Pile (Patio & Landscaping) – The perfect balance of softness and durability, ideal for decorative outdoor spaces.

High-Pile (Lawn Replacement) – A lush, natural-looking alternative to grass—without the watering, mowing, or upkeep.
